# Changelog

Each version gets a section like `## X.Y.Z [release]` (or `[beta]` or `[alpha]`). The release workflow sends that section's text and type to CurseForge.

## 0.7.1 [release]
- Every race has its own farts, with a separate set for each body type. That's 74 per voice across 18 voices, plus 5 Artifact farts, so 1337 in all.
- Farts have rarities like loot, from Poor up to Legendary (1 in 500). Artifact is 1 in 5000.
- Everyone near you with the addon hears the same fart, even when the person who farted doesn't have it.
- Spam protection plays one sound at a time per person.
- 34 achievements, and a lot of them are about farting with other people. 15 of them unlock a new emote.
- Your Collection saves every fart you hear, and the Board shows the top 10 most heard and top listeners.
- Random farts are on by default, about once an hour and when you get out of a sleeping bag. Type /wind random off if you don't want them.
- There's a Fart button, a fart log and the changelog in Options > AddOns > Spoken Wind.
